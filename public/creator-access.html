How creator access works

You stay in control of your work. We handle reader access, reporting, and revenue distribution through a clear partnership.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your catalog

    Share the titles you represent, the rights you control, and how you would like readers to discover your work.

  2. 02

    Choose your terms

    Review licensing, availability, and reporting details before any title is added to Free Bookery.

  3. 03

    Reach readers and earn

    Follow readership and engagement while receiving your share of eligible ad, sponsor, grant, and donation revenue.

Built around your rights

Access should not mean giving your work away.

  • You retain ownership. Your copyright stays yours.
  • You approve participation. No title goes live without permission.
  • You see the results. Readership and compensation are reported clearly.

Upload the book.

PDF and EPUB are the reader formats Free Bookery can validate and publish.

A file must open successfully and its first page must render into a visible preview so we can confirm it is readable—not blank, corrupted, encrypted, or unsupported. Files stay private during review.

Include ISBN, title, author/contributors, book-file location, and cover for each title. Language defaults to English and age range defaults to Adult. Categories, description, series, and edition are optional. An Admin will follow up about transferring the readable PDF/EPUB files.
