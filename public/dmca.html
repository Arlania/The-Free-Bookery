Dated: September 23, 2026

Free Bookery (“Company”) respects the intellectual property rights of others. We comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (“DMCA”) and other applicable intellectual property laws.

1. Notification of Infringement

If you believe that your copyrighted work has been copied in a way that constitutes copyright infringement and is accessible via the Service, please notify our Copyright Agent.

Your notification must include:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing and where it is located on the Service.
  4. Your contact information, including address, phone number, and email.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material is not authorized.
  6. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate.
